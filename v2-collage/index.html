The café comes to you.

Kin is a family-run espresso cart serving weddings, corporate gatherings, open houses, and private parties across Los Angeles — a Malibu bluff at golden hour, a Pasadena backyard on a slow Sunday, a rooftop downtown before the toasts begin. We arrive with everything: the cart, a practiced barista, and single-origin espresso crafted to order, so all you have to do is gather your people.

Licensed, insured, and fully self-contained — if there’s room for a good conversation, there’s room for us.

Meet Kin

Kristen & Adhir

Kin began the way most good things in our family do — around a table, over coffee, with everyone talking at once. We’re Kristen and Adhir, and we built this cart because the moments we remember from every celebration are the small ones: a warm cup pressed into a cold hand, a stranger becoming a friend in line, someone’s grandmother lingering at the cart for a second cappuccino.

We’re owner-operated and family-run, which means one of us is behind the cart at your event — not a franchise crew. We learn names. We remember orders. We treat your people like ours, because for a few hours, they are.

Good questions

What does the cart need for power?

One dedicated 110V, 15–20 amp outlet within about 25 feet of the cart — the standard household kind. It shouldn’t be shared with the DJ or lighting rig; espresso machines are thirsty. For venues without power, we bring a quiet generator.

Does the cart need a water hookup?

No — the cart is fully self-contained. We arrive with everything we need, water included.

How much space does the cart take up?

Roughly six by six feet — an eight-by-four-foot footprint is even more comfortable. The cart looks its best set against a wall or backdrop, but it’s happy anywhere your layout allows.

Can you set up indoors or outdoors? What about weather?

Both. Indoors, the cart rolls through any standard doorway. Outdoors, we serve rain or shine with a covered setup — and if severe weather forces a cancellation, we’ll reschedule in good faith.

How long do setup and breakdown take?

About 45 minutes to set up and 30 to break down — never counted against your service hours. You book the pouring time; the bookends are on us.

Are you insured?

Yes — we carry general liability insurance, and we’re glad to send a certificate of insurance directly to your venue on request.

How do booking and the deposit work?

A deposit holds your date, with the balance due about a week before the event. We recommend reaching out four to eight weeks ahead, though last-minute dates are always welcome when the calendar allows.

Do you travel outside Los Angeles?

Travel is included across LA and surrounding areas — Malibu to Pasadena, the Valley to Long Beach. Beyond that, a modest travel fee applies, always disclosed upfront in your proposal. Never a surprise.

Is there a guest minimum?

None. We’ve designed packages for intimate open houses and 300-cup receptions alike, and we bring the same care to both.
