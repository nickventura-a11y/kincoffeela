A coffee bar that comes to you

Kin is a family-run espresso bar on wheels, serving weddings, corporate gatherings, open houses and private parties across Los Angeles. We arrive quietly, set an elegant cart wherever the day is happening — a Malibu lawn at golden hour, a Pasadena courtyard, a conference floor in Culver City — and pour single-origin espresso, crafted to order, for as long as you have us.

We’re licensed and insured, and the cart is fully self-contained: power solved, water on board, setup and breakdown handled without a word. All you provide is the occasion. We treat your guest list the way we treat our own table.

The cart

A quiet piece of furniture, and the person behind it

The cart itself is warm stone and walnut, with softly lit shelves and a framed menu board — as much at home under string lights on a coastal lawn as it is beside a lobby wall at nine in the morning. It arrives styled, with its backdrop, its greenery and its own glow, and settles into a corner as if it had always been there.

Behind it stands a professional barista pulling locally roasted, single-origin espresso, one drink at a time — someone who learns names, remembers the oat-milk cappuccino from an hour ago, and keeps the line feeling less like a queue and more like a kitchen counter.

Always included — the styled cart and backdrop  ·  a professional barista  ·  a commercial espresso machine  ·  locally roasted, single-origin espresso  ·  cups, lids & sleeves  ·  small-batch syrups  ·  oat, almond & whole milk  ·  full setup and breakdown.

Questions, answered

What does the cart need for power?

One dedicated 110V outlet — standard household power, 15 to 20 amps — within about 25 feet of where the cart will stand, and not shared with the DJ or lighting. If your venue doesn’t have power where you’d like us, we can bring a quiet generator.

Do you need a water hookup?

No. The cart is fully self-contained — we bring our own water and everything else the bar needs, so there’s nothing for you or your venue to arrange.

How much space does the cart take up?

Roughly six feet by six — between 6×6 and 8×4 feet depending on the setup — ideally placed against a wall or backdrop so guests approach from the front. It fits comfortably in living rooms, lobbies, courtyards and lawns.

Can you set up indoors? What about weather?

Both, happily. Outdoors, we pour rain or shine with a covered setup. If severe weather cancels your event outright, we’ll work with you in good faith to reschedule.

How long do setup and breakdown take?

We arrive about 45 minutes before service to set up, and breakdown takes about 30 minutes after. Neither is ever counted against your service hours.

Are you insured?

Yes — Kin carries general liability insurance, and we’ll send a certificate of insurance directly to your venue on request.

How does booking work?

A deposit holds your date, and the balance is due about a week before your event. We recommend reaching out four to eight weeks ahead, though last-minute dates are always welcome when the calendar allows.

Do you travel outside Los Angeles?

Travel is included across LA and surrounding areas — Malibu to Pasadena, the Valley, and down into Orange County. Beyond that, a modest travel fee applies, always disclosed upfront in your proposal.

Is there a guest minimum?

None. Our packages are shaped for everything from an intimate open house to a 300-cup reception.
