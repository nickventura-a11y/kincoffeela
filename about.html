About us

Kristen & Adhir

Kin Coffee is the two of us — a family-run cart in the most literal sense. Adhir builds the menus and runs the bar; Kristen handles the logistics that make a five-hour event feel effortless.

We started Kin because the moments we remember from our own gatherings were never the grand ones. They were the pauses — someone pressing a warm cup into your hands, a conversation that ran a little longer than it meant to.

So when we pour at your event, it isn’t staff working a shift. It’s us, showing up the way family does — early, prepared, and glad to be there.

When we’re here, you’re family.
