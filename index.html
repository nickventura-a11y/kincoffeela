What we do

The café that comes to you

Kin Coffee is a family-run espresso cart serving weddings, corporate events, open houses, and private parties across Los Angeles and its surrounding areas. We arrive with everything — the cart, the barista, the beans — and set up wherever your day is happening: a Malibu bluff at golden hour, a Pasadena courtyard, a conference floor downtown.

Licensed, insured, and fully self-contained, we only need a corner and an outlet. You gather the people. We’ll pour.

No rentals to coordinate, no extras to chase down. If it belongs at a coffee bar, it arrives with us.
